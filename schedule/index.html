Common questions

Before you book

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. The 15-minute consultation is free and there's no obligation. It's simply a chance to ask questions and get a sense of whether working together feels like a good fit.

Who do you work with?

Adults ages 18–64, as individuals or couples. Common reasons people reach out include anxiety and stress, depression and low mood, relationship and communication challenges, and feeling stuck in patterns they can't quite untangle on their own.

Do you offer telehealth?

Yes. Secure video sessions are available to clients anywhere in Colorado. In-person sessions are offered at the Westminster office and in Boulder.

Where is the office?

8700 Turnpike Dr, Suite 400, Westminster, CO 80031. Boulder availability is shown in the online scheduler.

What if I have trouble with the scheduler?

Try opening it in a new tab, or simply call (719) 445-9294 or email kayyemtherapy@gmail.com and we'll find a time together.

What if I need help right now?

If you're in crisis or need immediate support, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or go to your nearest emergency room. This website and the scheduler aren't monitored for urgent messages.

Prefer to talk first?

Reach out however feels easiest.

Call, email, or send a message from the contact form — whichever is most comfortable for you.
