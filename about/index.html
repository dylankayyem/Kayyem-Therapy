About Me

Licensed Social Worker  ·  Colorado

Hi, I'm Allie Kayyem — a Licensed Social Worker (LSW) practicing in Westminster, Colorado. I work with adults and couples who feel stuck in patterns they can't quite untangle on their own. Therapy with me is collaborative, grounded, and focused on helping you understand yourself with more clarity and compassion.

My role is to walk alongside you — helping identify patterns, explore what's beneath the surface, and create space for meaningful change. I aim to offer a calm, nonjudgmental environment where you don't have to perform or have it all figured out.

I practice under clinical supervision as required by the state of Colorado.

My Therapeutic Approach

Therapy should be tailored to you — there is no one-size-fits-all approach. Depending on your needs and goals, I draw from a range of evidence-based modalities to guide our work together.

  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Identifying and shifting unhelpful thought patterns to improve mood and behavior.
  • Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT) Building emotional regulation, distress tolerance, and interpersonal effectiveness skills.
  • Trauma-Informed Care Creating a safe space to process past experiences with compassion and at your own pace.
  • Gottman Method Strengthening relationships through research-backed tools for communication and connection.
  • Narrative Therapy Exploring and rewriting the stories we tell about ourselves to reclaim a sense of agency.
  • Strengths-Based Interventions Building on your existing resilience and what already works well in your life.

Outside the Office

When I'm not in session, I enjoy spending time with my family and friends. I appreciate friendly competition — especially board games — that enrich skills of patience, humor, and connection that guide my work. I'm an avid reader who appreciates many different types of books in both fiction and nonfiction. I also value spending time outdoors, whether that's a hike, getting fresh air, or simply being present in nature. I strive to find moments of mindfulness to balance out the hustle of everyday life.
