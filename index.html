Who I work with

Feeling stuck is a good reason to start.

I work with adults and couples who feel caught in patterns they can't quite untangle on their own. Some of the things we might work on together:

Anxiety & stress

Quieting the constant hum of worry and finding steadier ground to stand on.

Depression & low mood

Reconnecting with energy, meaning, and the things that matter to you.

Relationships & communication

Saying what you mean, hearing what's underneath, and repairing after conflict.

Couples therapy

Research-backed Gottman Method tools for connection, trust, and feeling like a team again.

Patterns that keep repeating

Understanding where they come from and creating space for something new.

Past experiences

Processing what happened at your own pace, with a trauma-informed approach.

Meet your therapist

Hi, I'm Allie.

I'm a Licensed Social Worker practicing in Westminster, Colorado. Therapy with me is collaborative, grounded, and focused on helping you understand yourself with more clarity and compassion.

My role is to walk alongside you — helping identify patterns, explore what's beneath the surface, and create space for meaningful change.

I aim to offer a calm, nonjudgmental environment where you don't have to perform or have it all figured out. Reaching out can feel like the hardest step — if you're curious about working together, a free 15-minute consultation is an easy place to begin.

There's no one-size-fits-all therapy.

Depending on your needs and goals, I draw from several evidence-based approaches — always tailored to you, and at a pace that feels right.
